# Data availability

The empirical analysis uses licensed Norgate Data Australia Stocks Platinum data, including historical point-in-time ASX 200 membership and security-level market history. Those licensed source data cannot be redistributed through this repository.

Accordingly, this repository does **not** contain:

- the raw Norgate extraction;
- the point-in-time ASX 200 security-level panel;
- historical constituent-level datasets that could substitute for licensed membership access;
- row-level security-price histories derived from Norgate; or
- other security-level outputs excluded by the publication public-file whitelist.

The repository does provide redistributable aggregate evidence, validation artifacts, fold-level summaries, year-level tax-loss summaries, and diagnostic aggregate outputs that are sufficient to reproduce the manuscript tables, figures, and reported inferential conclusions without redistributing licensed market data.

A full empirical rerun requires lawful access to equivalent Norgate data and reconstruction of the publication panel according to the documented schema and point-in-time membership rules.

The manuscript evidence was frozen in the private development repository at commit:

`c835cd89e45a46b0b82356ef6b6d40334971da39`

Public evidence files are transferred from that frozen state unless explicitly identified as public-only reproducibility documentation or wrappers.
