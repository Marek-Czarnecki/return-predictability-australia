# Publication evidence classes

The public evidence package distinguishes the article's principal mechanism evidence from the broader confirmatory study and from diagnostic attribution that does not constitute the exact decomposition.

## A. Principal mechanism evidence

The revised article is centred on a matched seven-fold trend-following experiment that changes the historical universe treatment while holding vendor/security coverage and the evaluation calendar common.

For each fold:

- **A** = point-in-time universe + point-in-time-selected parameters;
- **B** = retrospective universe + retrospective-selected parameters;
- **C** = retrospective universe + point-in-time-selected parameters;
- **D** = point-in-time universe + retrospective-selected parameters.

A symmetric two-factor Shapley decomposition gives a universe/composition component and a parameter-selection component that sum exactly to `B - A` in every fold.

Across the seven folds:

- mean total treatment effect = `0.153051485153`;
- mean universe/composition component = `0.151701286408`;
- mean parameter-selection component = `0.001350198745`;
- the universe component is positive in 7/7 folds;
- selected parameters change in 5/7 folds.

The public fold-level values are in `data/evidence/publication_trend_2x2_decomposition.csv`.

## B. Cross-sectional concentration evidence

Security-level accounting is used only to diagnose whether the direct composition effect is dominated by a few securities. The public repository exposes aggregate concentration statistics, not security-level rows.

The reported aggregate results are:

- 718 contributing assets;
- top-1 absolute contribution share: 4.3709%;
- top-5: 15.3023%;
- top-10: 24.5583%;
- 28 securities required to reach 50% of absolute contribution;
- 81 securities required to reach 80%;
- absolute-contribution HHI: 0.0126978.

These statistics support the bounded conclusion that the composition distortion is cross-sectionally diffuse rather than generated by a handful of extreme survivors.

**Technical boundary:** security-level contributions reconcile exactly to the arithmetic daily net-return stream, not to compounded terminal NAV. They are not a linear decomposition of terminal NAV.

## C. Supporting confirmatory publication evidence

The broader study contains exactly four pre-defined confirmatory tests: trend following, mean reversion, pairs trading, and tax-loss selling. Holm family-wise multiplicity control is applied across this four-test family. None receives Holm-adjusted support under the publication-standard point-in-time design.

The definitive manuscript-facing confirmatory results are recorded in `data/evidence/publication_final_primary_results.csv`.

## D. Historical comparison and diagnostic attribution

The earlier frozen-capstone versus publication comparisons establish where evidentiary conclusions changed. The same-vendor membership ablation demonstrates that retrospective-current membership is a major contributor to the earlier trend discrepancy. Transaction costs are a smaller contributor; benchmark choice, metric semantics, risk-free treatment, and sample extension do not explain the deterioration; vendor/security coverage remains unresolved.

Those earlier diagnostic ablations are not themselves an additive causal decomposition. The exact two-factor decomposition described in Section A is a later, separate post-rejection originality analysis and should not be conflated with the earlier attribution table.

## Claim boundaries

The evidence does **not** support claims that:

- survivorship bias is newly discovered;
- this is the first evidence that survivorship affects Australian momentum;
- the controlled Norgate experiment fully explains the earlier Yahoo/current-constituent result;
- the four tested strategies universally "do not work" in Australia; or
- security-level arithmetic contributions linearly decompose compounded terminal NAV.
